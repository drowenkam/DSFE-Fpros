Specialist caveat: The glossary identifies standardized professional terminology, but it is not a replacement for trauma, infection control, implant or restorative treatment guidelines. Replacing “implant retained” with “implant supported,” or “centric relation” with “maximum intercuspation,” would change meaning. No such substitution was made.
